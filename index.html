02

A four-step workflow kept the work simple

Each step produced a clear handoff to the next one.

  1. 1

    Provide sources

    Share the articles, documents, screenshots, and links that contain the evidence.

  2. 2

    Approve the storyline

    Agree on the audience, main takeaway, and one sentence for each slide.

  3. 3

    Build and refine

    Let Codex write the HTML, CSS, navigation, and slide layouts.

  4. 4

    Review and publish

    Check meaning and rendering, then publish the static files.

The author makes the editorial decision before Codex starts production.

08

Meaning and rendering needed separate reviews

A good screenshot cannot prove that the argument is correct.

Meaning review

  • Read only the slide headlines in order
  • Check every claim against its source
  • Preserve uncertainty and scope
  • Remove detail that does not serve the reader

Editorial question: Does the deck ask the audience to believe the right thing?

Rendering review

  • Inspect every slide at presentation size
  • Test overflow and element bounds
  • Use the navigation controls from beginning to end
  • Check the deployed version before sharing it

Practical result: Browser review caught a content and footer collision before handoff.
